The rulebook

Eligibility is a derivation, not an opinion.

Every ARBEXEC verdict — ELIGIBLE, INELIGIBLE, or NEEDS REVIEW — is derived rule by rule from the federal IDR regulation, and the derivation is shown with the verdict. These are the rules the engine applies, in plain language.

Scope of the federal process

Federal IDR covers out-of-network emergency services, non-emergency services by an out-of-network provider at an in-network facility, and air ambulance services. Ground ambulance sits outside the federal categories — where a state process covers it, ARBEXEC routes the dispute there instead of discarding it. 45 CFR 149.510(a)(1)

Coverage it applies to

The process applies to group and individual health coverage, including self-funded plans. Workers' compensation, personal injury, Medicare, and Medicaid cases never enter the pipeline, under any configuration. 45 CFR 149.510(a)(2)

State law first

Where a specified state law provides its own payment-determination process, the state process governs and federal IDR does not apply. The screen checks this before anything else proceeds — and a state-law dispute is routed to the state process and captured, not dropped. 45 CFR 149.510(a)(2)(i)

A real payment dispute

IDR resolves payment disputes. If the plan's payment already meets or exceeds the billed amount, there is no dispute to file — the case stops here, with the reason recorded. 45 CFR 149.510(a)

Open negotiation comes first

Before IDR, the parties get a 30-business-day open negotiation period, opened by notice within 30 business days of the initial payment or notice of denial. ARBEXEC tracks the window from the remittance — not from memory. 45 CFR 149.510(b)(1)

The filing window is four business days

Once open negotiation ends without agreement, IDR must be initiated within 4 business days. It is the shortest window in the process, and the one manual preparation most often misses. 45 CFR 149.510(b)(2)

Cooling-off after a decision

After a decided dispute, a 90-day cooling-off period applies to the same parties for the same item or service. ARBEXEC flags affected cases for review rather than letting them file into a bar. 45 CFR 149.510

Batching same or similar items

Qualified items or services that are the same or similar may be batched into a single dispute under the federal batching rules, where the conditions are met. 45 CFR 149.510(b)(2)(ii)

No estimates, ever

The qualifying payment amount (QPA) comes from the plan's remittance. When it is absent, the verdict is NEEDS REVIEW — ARBEXEC never estimates a QPA, and never lets a guessed figure into a packet.
