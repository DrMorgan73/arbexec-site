Most eligible disputes are never filed.

The federal windows are short, the paperwork is exact, and preparing a case by hand is slow — so eligible out-of-network disputes get skipped, and the money the No Surprises Act already provides for is left on the table. ARBEXEC closes that gap: the preparation is the product.

Workflow

From remittance chaos to a filing-ready packet.

Six stages, one pipeline. Every case moves through the same guarded path — and a case can only move forward when the stage before it is complete.

Step 1 · Intake

Connected, or uploaded

Two ways in. Connect your EHR: ARBEXEC pulls patient, encounter, coverage, claim, and payment context over FHIR R4 with SMART authorization, and cross-checks every claim against it. Or upload manually — 837 and 835 files, CSV, PDF remittances, and scanned paper, read by local OCR and fed into the same gated pipeline.

Step 2 · Eligibility

A cited screen, every claim

Every claim is screened through eleven rule blocks. Each verdict — eligible, ineligible, or needs review — carries the rule and the citation behind it. A missing qualifying payment amount is flagged for review, never estimated. Workers' compensation, personal injury, Medicare, and Medicaid never enter the pipeline. 45 CFR 149.510

Step 3 · Negotiation

Open negotiation, tracked

The 30-business-day open negotiation window runs from the initial payment or denial, with the deadline always visible and a breach flagged the day it happens. 45 CFR 149.510(b)(1)

Step 4 · Packet

Packet assembly

The filing packet is assembled as an evidence document with a hash-verified manifest — what was handed off can be proven later, byte for byte.

Step 5 · Handoff

Certified-filer handoff

ARBEXEC prepares; a certified IDR entity files. The handoff is a first-class, tracked step — not an email attachment and a hope.

Step 6 · Outcome

Outcome & payment tracking

Decisions, payment deadlines, and enforcement flags are tracked to closure. A breached deadline surfaces — it never silently rolls forward.

Capture

The hunting layer: find every dispute you are owed.

Screening what you are handed is only half the work. The capture layer sweeps your remittances themselves — whole files at a time — and surfaces the disputes manual review walks past.

Cohort sweep

Drop in a whole 835 remittance file — or a month of them. Every claim in the batch is screened through the same cited eligibility engine, and every captured dispute lands on the cohort board with its route: federal IDR, state process, or excluded, with the reason recorded.

Underpayment detection

Paid below the payer's own stated QPA, paid at zero, or paid at a fraction of the billed amount — each pattern is flagged as a dispute candidate, with the arithmetic shown on the signal.

Downcoding & bundling

When a payer quietly re-codes a line to a lower-paying code, or folds a service into another line's payment, the remittance itself says so. ARBEXEC reads those signals line by line and flags them as disputes in their own right.

Denial-code classification

Every adjustment and remark code (CARC/RARC) on the remittance is classified — payment-dispute candidate, bundling candidate, clinical denial, administrative — with the reason written down. A code the system does not know is never guessed at; it goes to review.

Deadline radar

Every open case is aged against its windows — negotiation ending, initiation deadline, filer acknowledgment, payment window — and ordered by urgency, including cases still in intake that no board shows yet. Nothing is found after its window has closed.

A wider net, honestly drawn

Air ambulance disputes screen as the federal category they are. Ground ambulance and fully-insured disputes in specified-state-law states are routed to the state process and captured — not discarded. Workers' compensation, personal injury, Medicare, and Medicaid stay excluded under every configuration. 45 CFR 149.510(a)(1)

Why ARBEXEC

Why this works

IDR preparation fails in predictable places: cases get cherry-picked, deadlines slip quietly, figures get guessed. ARBEXEC is built against exactly those failures.

No cherry-picking

Every claim that comes in is screened, and every eligible case moves forward. The engine does not pick winners — the rules decide, and the verdict is written down with its citation.

Cited, not guessed

Each eligibility verdict carries its rule ID and its CFR citation. Billed, paid, and qualifying payment amounts come from your remittances — a figure that is absent is flagged for review, never invented.

Deadlines are load-bearing

A federal-holiday-aware business-day calendar drives every window — open negotiation, IDR initiation, cooling-off. Breached and near-deadline cases surface first, breached first.

Your data stays behind the gate

Case data is held in IEC's dedicated, access-gated database — never on a public site, never in a shared pool. Document scanning and OCR run locally, and no third-party scanning service ever touches a claim. The application itself is access-gated — unique individual accounts, role-based permissions, passwords stored only as hashes, and TLS on every connection.

Human-verified figures

Extraction proposes; people dispose. Billed, paid, and qualifying payment amounts are verified by a human reviewer before any packet is finalized — the machine never gets the last word on a number that goes to a filer.

FAQ

Clear answers about ARBEXEC.

Does ARBEXEC file our cases?

No. ARBEXEC prepares filing-ready packets and tracks each case end to end; the filing itself is performed by a certified IDR entity. That separation is by design — the preparer and the filer are different parties.

Which disputes does the federal IDR process cover?

Out-of-network emergency services, non-emergency services by an out-of-network provider at an in-network facility, and air ambulance services, under 45 CFR 149.510. Where a specified state law applies, the state process governs instead — the ARBEXEC screen checks that first, and routes ground-ambulance and fully-insured state-law disputes to the state process rather than discarding them.

Do all of our cases get screened, or only the strong ones?

All of them. ARBEXEC screens every claim it takes in and moves every eligible case forward. Cases are never cherry-picked, and a case that needs review is flagged with the reason — never quietly dropped.

Where does our claims data go?

Nowhere it shouldn't. Case data is held in IEC's dedicated, access-gated database; document scanning and OCR run locally, and no third-party scanning service is used. The ARBEXEC operator system sits behind an access gate — it is never a public site.
