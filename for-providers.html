For providers

For hospitals and practices with out-of-network disputes.

ARBEXEC is built for the organizations the No Surprises Act protects on the provider side — and for the billing teams who carry the preparation today.

Hospitals & health systems

High out-of-network volume across emergency and facility-based services, where the 4-business-day filing window makes manual preparation a losing race.

Physician practices

Practices whose out-of-network cases are individually small and collectively significant — exactly the cases cherry-picking leaves behind. ARBEXEC screens them all.

Billing & revenue teams

The teams who track negotiation windows in spreadsheets today get a cohort board instead: every case, its stage, its deadline, and its cited verdict, in one place.

What you hand over. What you get back.

Intake runs on what your systems already produce. Nothing new to key in.

What you hand over

Claims and remittances — through an EHR-connected pull, electronic 835/837 files, PDF documents, or paper. Billed, paid, and qualifying payment amounts are read from the remittance and verified by a human reviewer.

What you get back

A cited eligibility verdict for every claim; open-negotiation tracking with deadlines that surface before they breach; filing-ready packets handed to a certified IDR entity; and an outcome board that follows each case through decision to payment.

The data boundary

IDR work is identified work — a filing names the provider, the payer, and the claim. ARBEXEC keeps that data inside your own environment: local scanning and OCR, storage encrypted at rest, and an operator application behind an access gate. Your claims are never uploaded to a third-party scanning cloud, and no case data ever appears on this public site.
